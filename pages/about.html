About TravelAI

Making travel planning more personal, intelligent and effortless.

Our Vision

Travel Planning, Reimagined

TravelAI is a tourism-focused intelligent travel recommendation and itinerary generation system. Instead of requiring travelers to search through countless websites and manually organize their plans, TravelAI brings destination discovery, recommendations, activities, accommodation, weather and itinerary planning into one platform.

The system is designed around the traveler's preferences, interests, budget and trip duration to create a more personalized planning experience.
